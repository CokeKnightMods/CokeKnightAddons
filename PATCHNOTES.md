# CokeKnightAddons 0.17.4 — Performance Update

- **90–175% higher average FPS** compared with 0.17.3 in the tested scenes.
- **AWP gameplay:** 188 → 413 FPS (**+120%**).
- **Full inventory:** 118 → 279 FPS (**+137%**).
- **Frequent item switching:** 291 → 554 FPS (**+90%**).
- **Multiple weapon models and active inspect animations:** 36 → 100 FPS (**+175%**).
- Improved 1% lows and lower P99 frame times across all four scenes.
- No reductions to model detail, texture quality or animation fidelity.
- Existing settings, loadouts and downloaded assets remain compatible and available offline.

Results are averages of three runs per scene at 1280×720 on a Ryzen 7 7800X3D and RTX 5070 Ti, with VSync and FPS limits disabled. Percentage gains use unrounded measurements. Local benchmarks do not guarantee identical gains on Hypixel or with other mod combinations. Active shader packs were not performance-benchmarked. Average peak process RAM was 82–126 MiB higher than 0.17.3; this update does not claim lower RAM usage.
